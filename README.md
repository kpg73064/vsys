## Who Actually Uses VSYS Host?

The short answer: privacy-conscious users, developers who want root access without babysitting, businesses that need offshore hosting where DMCA takedowns don't apply, and anyone tired of explaining to their bank why they're paying a Ukrainian hosting company.

Since VSYS servers are located outside the US, they don't respond to DMCA takedown requests. That's not a wink-and-nudge thing — it's just how offshore hosting jurisdictions work, and VSYS is pretty upfront about it. It's why it's popular with hot-topic blogs, free speech platforms, and international businesses targeting European and American audiences while staying anonymous.

The no-KYC angle is real too. You can sign up without providing personal data and pay entirely in cryptocurrency — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, and more. Traditional payment methods (credit card, PayPal, wire transfer) work fine too if that's your thing.
